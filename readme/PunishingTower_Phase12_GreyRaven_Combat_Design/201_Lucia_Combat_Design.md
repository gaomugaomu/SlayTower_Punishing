# Lucia Combat Design

Role: Attack Type

Identity: Red orb specialist.

## Normal Attack

Cost: 1 Action

Effect: Deal 6 damage. Gain 1 energy.

## Red Orb

1 Red: Deal 4 damage.

2 Red: Deal 9 damage.

3 Red: Deal 18 damage and gain Core Mark.

## Blue Orb

1 Blue: Gain 2 energy.

2 Blue: Gain 4 energy.

3 Blue: Gain 6 energy.

## Yellow Orb

1 Yellow: Gain 3 shield.

2 Yellow: Gain 6 shield.

3 Yellow: Gain 10 shield.

## Core Passive

After three match: The next red orb activation receives bonus damage.

## Ultimate

Cost: 100 energy + 1 action

Effect: Deal 40 damage to all enemies.

## Leader Skill

First red orb each battle gains increased damage.
