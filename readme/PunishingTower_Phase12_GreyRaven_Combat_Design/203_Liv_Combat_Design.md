# Liv Combat Design

Role: Support Type

Identity: Healing and utility.

## Normal Attack

Deal 3 damage. Gain energy.

## Red Orb

1: 5 damage

2: 10 damage

3: 18 damage

## Blue Orb

1: Heal 3 HP

2: Heal 6 HP

3: Heal 12 HP

## Yellow Orb

1: Gain 5 shield

2: Gain 10 shield

3: Gain 18 shield

## Core Passive

Improves first support action each battle.

## Ultimate

Heal 25 HP and remove one negative effect.

## Leader Skill

First battle turn: Draw 2 additional blue orbs.
