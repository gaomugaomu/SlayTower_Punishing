# Lee Combat Design

Role: Attack Type

Identity: Flexible damage dealer.

## Normal Attack

Deal 5 damage. Gain energy.

## Red Orb

1: 5 damage

2: 11 damage

3: 22 damage

## Blue Orb

1: Draw 1 orb

2: Draw 2 orbs

3: Draw 3 orbs

## Yellow Orb

1: Gain 4 shield

2: Gain 8 shield

3: Gain 12 shield

## Core Passive

After using yellow orb: next attack gains bonus effect.

## Ultimate

Deal 35 damage and weaken enemy.

## Leader Skill

Increase orb generation consistency.
